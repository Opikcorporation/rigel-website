The problem

Real estate agencies in Dubai operate with fragmented systems

Leads arrive from portals like Property Finder, Bayut or advertising campaigns. Agents handle conversations on WhatsApp. Contracts and commissions are managed separately.

Managers struggle to see what is happening inside the team.

Deals get lost. Follow-ups are inconsistent. Performance becomes difficult to measure.

The result is simple: lost opportunities, operational chaos and limited visibility over sales performance.

Artificial Intelligence

Rigel Intelligence

Not a chatbot. Not a dashboard summary. An embedded AI engine that processes your commercial data in real time, identifies patterns humans miss, and delivers decision-ready insights exactly when they matter.

Intelligent lead matching

Analyzes client profiles against hundreds of data points, budget, lifestyle, investment goals, market timing, to surface the most relevant projects instantly.

Example: "Client prefers sea view, 2BR, under 3M" → 14 matching projects ranked by conversion probability

Real-time sales coaching

During live interactions, provides agents with contextual objection handling, competitive positioning, and market data to close deals faster.

Example: Client says "too expensive" → AI suggests comparable projects, ROI projections, and payment plan options in real time

Predictive performance analytics

Goes beyond reporting. Predicts pipeline bottlenecks, flags underperforming segments, and recommends team allocation before problems escalate.

Example: "Team B conversion dropped 12% this week. Cause: 3 high-value leads stalled at proposal stage. Suggested: reassign to senior agents."

Strategic campaign steering

Dynamically adjusts team focus based on organizational priorities, inventory levels, and commission structures. The AI decides what your team should sell next.

Example: Developer X has 8 unsold units with 6% commission → AI prioritizes this project in lead assignments and agent dashboards

Always-on Telegram access

Query Rigel Intelligence from anywhere via Telegram. Get instant answers on lead status, pipeline data, team performance, and market intelligence on the move.

Example: "@rigel how many leads converted this week?" → "47 leads converted. Top performer: Sarah (12). Revenue: AED 2.3M."

Deep market intelligence

Continuously processes market data, pricing trends, competitor launches, and inventory movements. Every recommendation is grounded in live market context.

Example: "Marina area prices up 8% in 30 days. 3 new launches incoming. Suggest accelerating current Marina leads before price adjustment."
