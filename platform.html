Artificial Intelligence

Rigel Intelligence

An embedded AI engine woven into every layer of the platform. Rigel Intelligence observes patterns, connects data points, and surfaces decision-ready insights that drive better commercial outcomes.

Intelligent lead matching

Cross-references client data, budget, preferences, investment timeline, behavior signals, against your entire project inventory to generate ranked recommendations.

Example: Client opens 3 waterfront listings in 48h → AI auto-generates a shortlist of 8 matching projects with availability

Real-time objection handling

Supports agents during live client interactions with contextual responses, market comparisons, and competitive data to overcome objections instantly.

Example: "Price is too high" → AI pulls 3 comparable sold units, ROI over 3 years, and flexible payment structures

Predictive performance insights

Generates actionable reports on team output, pipeline health, and conversion trends, and predicts issues before they become problems.

Example: "Agent conversion rate dropping, likely cause: lead quality in assigned segment. Suggest: rotate to high-intent queue."

Always-on Telegram access

Available on Telegram for on-the-go operational queries. Ask about pipeline status, lead data, team KPIs, or market conditions from anywhere.

Example: "@rigel status on the Al Barsha leads?" → "12 active, 3 in negotiation, projected close: AED 1.8M this month."

Deep market intelligence

Understands real estate market dynamics and factors live pricing data, competitor launches, and inventory shifts into every recommendation.

Example: "Downtown supply up 15%, recommend shifting focus to JBR where inventory is tightening and prices are rising."

Strategic campaign steering

Helps administrators orient team focus toward priority projects or campaigns, dynamically adjusting based on inventory and commission structures.

Example: Developer launches Phase 2 with 7% commission → AI auto-prioritizes this project across all agent dashboards
